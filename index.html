About

who

I'm Ridhwan Roshan. I grew up watching Sneakers and The Matrix on repeat, convinced that breaking into things for a living was the coolest job that would never actually exist. Turns out it does, and I've been doing it for years: leading red team engagements end to end, from social engineering the front desk to pulling Domain Admin out of a misconfigured certificate authority.

Now AI is rewriting the field in real time, and honestly, it feels like the Matrix is happening as we speak. I spend most of my time red-teaming AI-backed enterprise applications, internal assistants and agentic workflows, finding the ways that untrusted content, over-trusted tools and weak identity turn a helpful copilot into an attack surface.

I never had much fascination for writing blog posts. It always felt like the part of the job that took longer than the actual hacking. Then AI changed that too. Most of the heavy-lift writing on this site was done with the help of Claude, and I'm genuinely grateful for it. So: thank you to Claude for making a terrible blogger into a slightly less terrible one, and thank you to every organisation I've worked with for giving me the stories worth writing about.

Ghost Sec Writes is where those stories land, written for educational purposes, from the assessments I've delivered across data centres, automotive, financial services and other sectors.
